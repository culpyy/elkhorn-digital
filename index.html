What's in it

Built around how a shop actually runs.

Every Timberline site starts with the things customers call you about, so they can answer those questions themselves.

01

Customers check their own build.

Every job gets a tracking code when it comes in. Your customer types it in and sees the stage, the progress and your latest note. You update it from your admin in a couple of taps, and the phone stops ringing with "is it done yet?"

02

A live board of what's on the bench.

A public page shows how many jobs are in the shop, in the queue and ready for pickup. It's proof you're busy and doing the work, and it fills in on its own as you update builds.

03

Mail-in work, without the mystery box.

Customers shipping you a firearm or a kit fill out two short steps and get a reference code to put in the box. When it shows up, you already know what it is and who sent it.

Dealer license checks

Look up a dealer's license against the ATF's list before anything ships. Your customers can search for a dealer near them too.

Reviews you can trust

When a build is ready, invite that customer to review it. Nothing shows on your site until you approve it.

News from the shop

Post what's new, what just came in or what you're working on. It shows on your homepage right away.

One login to run it

Update builds, post news, approve reviews, ask me for a change, see who's visiting and download all your data whenever you want.

Pricing

Two ways to pay. Same site.

You get the same site and the same tools either way. The only difference is how you pay for building it.

Low start

The smallest first bill

You pay a small start fee, and the rest of the build is spread into your monthly payment. There's one minimum: stay until the build is paid off, which is 12 months on Starter, 20 on Pro and 28 on Elite. If you leave sooner, you just pay what's left of the build. Nothing more.

Good fit if you'd rather keep cash in the shop right now.

Pay in full

The lowest monthly

You pay for the build up front, so your monthly is lower from day one. There's no minimum, and you can leave whenever you want.

Good fit if you plan to stay a while. The lower monthly makes up the bigger first payment in about a year on Starter, 20 months on Pro and 28 months on Elite.
