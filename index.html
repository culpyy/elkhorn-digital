What I built for Golden Valley Guns

Golden Valley Guns is a firearms manufacturer and gunsmith in Golden Valley, Arizona. Their business doesn't work like a normal online store. Every build goes through stages, customers mail in their own parts, and selling a gun involves legal steps. Off-the-shelf store software can't handle that, so I built them a system that does.

Visit the live site

A store that takes real payments

Over 13,000 products from the shop's distributors, with search, filters and live stock. Customers check out by card. Firearms get shipped to the customer's local licensed dealer, and the site handles that step.

Customers can check on their own build

When a job comes in, it gets a tracking code. Customers type it in and see exactly where their build is, from parts ordered through machining and finishing to ready for pickup. There's also a public board showing everything in the shop, so nobody has to call to ask.

Mailing something in is simple

Customers who send in a kit or a firearm fill out a short form and get a reference code to put in the box, so the shop knows what showed up and who it's from. For firearm sales, customers pick their receiving dealer from a search of the ATF's list of licensed dealers.

One place for the owner to run it all

From a single login, the owner manages builds, orders and refunds, customer messages, reviews, the photo gallery and the product catalog.
