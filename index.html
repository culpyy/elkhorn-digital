02Timberline

Built around how a shop actually runs.

Every site starts with the things customers call you about, so they can answer those questions themselves.

  1. Customers check their own build.

    Every job gets a tracking code. Your customer types it in and sees the stage, the progress and your latest note. The phone stops ringing with "is it done yet?"

  2. A live board of what's on the bench.

    A public page shows jobs in the shop, in the queue and ready for pickup. It fills in on its own as you update builds.

  3. Mail-in work, without the mystery box.

    Customers fill out two short steps and get a reference code for the box. When it shows up, you already know what it is and who sent it.

  4. Dealer license checks.

    Check a dealer against the ATF's list before anything ships. Customers can find a dealer near them too.

  5. Reviews you approve.

    Invite a customer to review a finished build. Nothing shows on your site until you say so.

  6. One login to run it.

    Update builds, post news, approve reviews, ask me for a change and download all your data whenever you want.

04Pricing

Two ways to pay. Same site.

Low start

You pay a small start fee, and the rest of the build is spread into your monthly payment. There's one minimum: stay until the build is paid off, which is 12 months on Starter, 20 on Pro and 28 on Elite. If you leave sooner, you just pay what's left of the build, plus your monthly through the 30-day notice.

Pay in full

You pay for the build up front, so your monthly is lower from day one. There's no minimum. You can leave any time with 30 days' notice. The lower monthly makes up the bigger first payment in about a year on Starter, 20 months on Pro and 28 months on Elite.
