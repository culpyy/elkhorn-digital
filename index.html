What I built for Golden Valley Guns

Golden Valley Guns builds and repairs firearms in Golden Valley, Arizona. A normal online store doesn't work for them. Every build goes through stages, customers mail in their own parts, and selling a gun comes with legal steps. Nothing off the shelf covers all of that, so I built it from scratch.

Visit the live site

An online store with real checkout

More than 13,000 products from the shop's distributors, with search, filters and live stock counts. Customers pay by card. Firearms ship to the buyer's local licensed dealer, and the site searches the ATF's list of licensed dealers so they can find theirs.

Customers can see where their build is

Every job gets a tracking code when it comes in. Customers enter it and see which stage their build is at, from parts ordered to machining to ready for pickup. A public board shows everything in the shop, so nobody has to call and ask.

Mailing in a build takes two short steps

Customers sending in a kit or a firearm fill out a short form and get a reference code for the box. When it arrives, the shop knows what it is and who it's from.

One dashboard runs the shop

From a single login, the owner handles builds, orders and refunds, customer messages, reviews, the photo gallery and the product catalog.
