Privacy

Last updated September 28, 2026

This site is run by Elkhorn Digital, LLC, a Nebraska company. Here's what happens to the information you give me through it.

What I collect

The quote form asks for your name, email, business name, current website, and a description of what you need. You choose what to put in. When you press "Request a quote," the details are sent to me by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form service that passes the message along to my inbox. I don't store form submissions anywhere else.

If you email me directly at braeden.culp@elkhorndigital.com, I get your email address and whatever you write.

How I use it

I use what you send to reply to you, talk through your project, and write your quote. If we work together, it becomes part of the project record. I don't sell your information, rent it, or share it with advertisers.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Other services this site uses

This site loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address when a page loads. FormSubmit handles your quote request on its way to me, under its own privacy policy. The site itself is hosted by a third party that may keep standard server logs, like IP addresses and the pages requested. I don't use those logs to identify anyone.

How long I keep it

I keep emails about a quote or project for as long as I need them to do the work and keep business records. If we never work together, you can ask me to delete your messages.

Your choices

You can ask me what I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email braeden.culp@elkhorndigital.com and I'll take care of it.

Changes

If I change how this site handles information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
